# Barometer

## What is a Barometer?

A Barometer is an instrument used to measure the magnitude of air pressure (atmospheric pressure) in a certain environment or surrounding.

## Uses of a Barometer

A barometer has widespread use in various physical sciences like physics and astronomy. Some of its practical applications include:
1. Measuring relative altitude and elevation changes.
2. Making short-term weather forecasts.
3. Conducting surface weather analysis.

## Digital Barometer in PSLab

### Materials Required
- Your Phone or Computer
- The **PSLab App** installed

### Procedure

1. Open the **PSLab app** on your device.
2. Navigate to the **Instruments** page and scroll down until you reach the **Barometer** option.
3. Tap on the instrument to open it.
4. The app will immediately begin reading the atmospheric pressure using your device's built-in barometer sensor (if available) or an external connected sensor.
5. You can view the data dynamically plotted on the graph and use it for altitude tracking or weather predictions.
